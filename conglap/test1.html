CHOOSING THE RIGHT PATH AFTER SCHOOL

For many students finishing secondary school, choosing between an academic path and a vocational path is not always easy. Some students discover their strengths early, while others need more time.

Extracurricular clubs can help with this. When students join a coding club or a debate team, they often find out what they are naturally good at. This kind of discovery is valuable. It helps students avoid a path that seems safe or conventional, but does not truly suit them.

Teachers say that curious students who try new activities tend to feel more confident about their decisions later. This willingness to explore helps them build a clearer picture of their own strengths and weaknesses.

Not every student needs the same path. Some succeed through university study, while others do well by taking up vocational courses instead. What matters most is that students stay committed and remain curious about new opportunities.
